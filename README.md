**Тип 3 № 3**  
**Грамматика**  
*Текст:*  
Last weekend, Anna A _____ (go) to the park. She B _____ (see) her friend there. They C _____ (play) football. Anna D _____ (can/not) find her ball, but her friend E _____ (help) her.  

**Варианты:**  
A) 1) go 2) went 3) going  
B) 1) see 2) saw 3) sees  
C) 1) play 2) played 3) plays  
D) 1) can’t 2) couldn’t 3) can not  
E) 1) help 2) helped 3) helps
